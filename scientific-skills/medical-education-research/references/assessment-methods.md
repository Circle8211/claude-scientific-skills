# Assessment Methods in Medical Education

## Assessment Framework

### Purposes of Assessment

| Purpose | Timing | Characteristics |
|---------|--------|-----------------|
| **Formative** | During learning | Low-stakes, feedback-focused |
| **Summative** | End of unit | High-stakes, decisions |
| **Diagnostic** | Before learning | Identify gaps, baseline |
| **Programmatic** | Longitudinal | Multiple data points, holistic |

## Written Assessments

### Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)

**Item Formats**:

1. **Single Best Answer (SBA)**
   ```
   A 45-year-old woman presents with crushing chest pain radiating
   to her left arm. ECG shows ST elevation in leads V1-V4.
   Which of the following is the most appropriate initial management?

   A. Aspirin and sublingual nitroglycerin
   B. Immediate cardiac catheterization
   C. Thrombolytic therapy
   D. Observation and serial troponins
   E. Echocardiography

   Answer: A (followed by B in STEMI protocol)
   ```

2. **Extended Matching Questions (EMQs)**
   ```
   Theme: Causes of Anemia

   Options:
   A. Iron deficiency anemia
   B. Vitamin B12 deficiency
   C. Folate deficiency
   D. Anemia of chronic disease
   E. Hemolytic anemia
   F. Sickle cell disease
   G. Thalassemia
   H. Aplastic anemia

   For each patient, select the most likely diagnosis:

   1. A 32-year-old vegetarian with fatigue and paresthesias...
   2. A 55-year-old with rheumatoid arthritis and normocytic anemia...
   ```

3. **Script Concordance Test (SCT)**
   ```
   Clinical Scenario: A 60-year-old smoker with chronic cough
   presents with hemoptysis.

   If you were thinking of:     And you found:          This hypothesis becomes:
   Lung cancer                  Normal chest X-ray      -2 -1 0 +1 +2
   ```

**Item Writing Guidelines**:
- Stem should present complete clinical scenario
- Avoid negative phrasing ("all EXCEPT")
- Make options grammatically parallel
- Avoid "always" or "never"
- Randomize correct answer position
- Avoid "all of the above" or "none of the above"

**Quality Metrics**:
| Metric | Target | Interpretation |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| Difficulty (p) | 0.3-0.7 | Proportion correct |
| Discrimination | >0.2 | High scorers correct more |
| Point-biserial | >0.15 | Correlation with total |

### Short Answer Questions (SAQs)

**Example**:
```
A 28-year-old woman presents with polyuria and polydipsia.
Fasting glucose is 280 mg/dL.

a) State the most likely diagnosis. (1 mark)
b) List THREE investigations you would order. (3 marks)
c) Describe TWO acute complications of this condition. (4 marks)
d) Outline the key principles of initial management. (4 marks)
```

**Marking Scheme**:
- Create detailed rubric
- Define acceptable alternatives
- Specify partial credit rules
- Train and calibrate markers

## Clinical Skills Assessment

### Objective Structured Clinical Examination (OSCE)

**Station Design**:
```
Station: Abdominal Examination

Duration: 10 minutes (8 min encounter + 2 min feedback)
Setting: Simulated clinic room

Candidate Instructions:
"This patient presents with abdominal pain.
Please perform an abdominal examination and
present your findings to the examiner."

Examiner Checklist:
□ Introduces self
□ Explains procedure
□ Positions patient correctly
□ Inspects abdomen systematically
□ Palpates all quadrants
□ Assesses for organomegaly
□ Performs special tests appropriately
□ Summarizes findings accurately

Global Rating:
1 - Below expectations
2 - Borderline
3 - Meets expectations
4 - Exceeds expectations
```

**OSCE Blueprinting**:
| Station | Competency | Content | Format |
|---------|------------|---------|--------|
| 1 | History | Chest pain | SP |
| 2 | Physical exam | Cardiac | Mannequin |
| 3 | Communication | Breaking bad news | SP |
| 4 | Procedural | IV insertion | Task trainer |
| 5 | Data interpretation | ECG/Labs | Written |

**Standard Setting for OSCEs**:
- Borderline regression method
- Borderline group method
- Contrasting groups method

### Standardized Patients (SPs)

**Training Components**:
1. Case portrayal consistency
2. Checklist completion accuracy
3. Feedback delivery skills
4. Emotional authenticity

**Validation**:
- Calibration sessions
- Inter-rater reliability checks
- Periodic retraining
- Quality assurance audits

## Workplace-Based Assessment (WBA)

### Mini-Clinical Evaluation Exercise (Mini-CEX)

**Domains Assessed**:
- History taking
- Physical examination
- Clinical reasoning
- Communication skills
- Professionalism
- Organization/efficiency
- Overall clinical competence

**Rating Scale**: 1-9 with anchors
- 1-3: Below expectations
- 4-6: Meets expectations
- 7-9: Above expectations

**Feedback Format**:
1. Strengths observed
2. Areas for improvement
3. Action plan agreed

### Direct Observation of Procedural Skills (DOPS)

**Procedure Examples**:
- IV cannulation
- Lumbar puncture
- Central line placement
- Suturing
- Intubation

**Assessment Criteria**:
- Indication and consent
- Preparation and technique
- Asepsis
- Technical ability
- Post-procedure care
- Communication

### Case-Based Discussion (CBD)

**Structure**:
1. Trainee presents case
2. Discussion of decision-making
3. Exploration of knowledge gaps
4. Feedback and action plan

**Focus Areas**:
- Clinical reasoning
- Investigation interpretation
- Management decisions
- Ethical considerations
- Documentation quality

### Multi-Source Feedback (360-degree)

**Raters**:
- Supervising physicians
- Nurses
- Allied health professionals
- Peers
- Patients
- Administrative staff

**Domains**:
- Communication
- Teamwork
- Professionalism
- Leadership
- Clinical care

## Programmatic Assessment

### Principles

1. **Triangulation**: Multiple assessment methods
2. **Longitudinal**: Assessment over time
3. **Low-stakes individual assessments**: High-stakes decisions from aggregate
4. **Meaningful feedback**: Focus on growth
5. **Competency committees**: Expert judgment for progression

### Implementation

```
Individual Assessment Events
        ↓
    Data Collection
        ↓
  Portfolio/Dashboard
        ↓
Competency Committee Review
        ↓
  Progression Decision
```

### Assessment Portfolio

**Contents**:
- Mini-CEX records
- DOPS assessments
- Case logs
- Reflective entries
- Feedback summaries
- Milestone ratings
- EPA entrustment levels

## Simulation-Based Assessment

### Simulation Modalities

| Type | Fidelity | Uses |
|------|----------|------|
| Task trainers | Low-medium | Procedural skills |
| Mannequin-based | Medium-high | ACLS, emergencies |
| Virtual reality | Variable | Anatomy, surgery |
| Standardized patients | High | Communication, history |
| Hybrid | High | Combined scenarios |

### Debriefing Assessment

**Observation Tools**:
- Debriefing Assessment for Simulation in Healthcare (DASH)
- Objective Structured Assessment of Debriefing (OSAD)

### Simulation Scenario Design

```
Scenario Title: Anaphylaxis Management

Learning Objectives:
- Recognize anaphylaxis
- Initiate appropriate treatment
- Demonstrate team communication

Setup:
- Mannequin with vital sign display
- Emergency equipment available
- Confederates as nurse, family member

Expected Actions:
- Call for help
- Administer epinephrine
- Establish airway
- IV access and fluids
- Monitor and reassess

Debriefing Points:
- Recognition timing
- Treatment priorities
- Team dynamics
- Crisis resource management
```

## Assessment Quality

### Validity Evidence (Messick's Framework)

| Source | Methods |
|--------|---------|
| **Content** | Expert review, blueprinting |
| **Response process** | Think-aloud, timing data |
| **Internal structure** | Factor analysis, reliability |
| **Relations to other variables** | Correlation with criteria |
| **Consequences** | Impact studies, pass rates |

### Reliability Metrics

**Test Reliability**:
- Cronbach's alpha (internal consistency)
- Test-retest reliability
- Parallel forms reliability

**Rater Reliability**:
- Inter-rater reliability
- Intra-rater reliability
- Intraclass correlation coefficient

**Generalizability Theory**:
- Identify variance sources
- Optimize assessment design
- Determine sampling adequacy

### Standard Setting Methods

| Method | Process | Best For |
|--------|---------|----------|
| Angoff | Expert judges estimate % correct | MCQs |
| Ebel | Categorize items by difficulty/relevance | MCQs |
| Borderline regression | Regress scores on global ratings | OSCEs |
| Contrasting groups | Define passing at group intersection | Various |
| Hofstee | Compromise method | When consensus difficult |
