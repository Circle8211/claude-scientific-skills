# Validated Psychological Instruments

## Depression Measures

### Patient Health Questionnaire-9 (PHQ-9)

**Purpose**: Screen for and measure severity of depression

**Items**: 9 items based on DSM criteria

**Response Format**: 0-3 scale (Not at all to Nearly every day)

**Scoring**:
| Score | Severity |
|-------|----------|
| 0-4 | Minimal |
| 5-9 | Mild |
| 10-14 | Moderate |
| 15-19 | Moderately severe |
| 20-27 | Severe |

**Psychometrics**:
- Cronbach's α = .89
- Test-retest r = .84
- Sensitivity: 88%
- Specificity: 88%

**Cut-off**: 10 commonly used for screening

### Beck Depression Inventory-II (BDI-II)

**Purpose**: Assess severity of depression

**Items**: 21 items

**Response Format**: 0-3 scale with unique anchors per item

**Scoring**: Sum of all items (0-63)
| Score | Severity |
|-------|----------|
| 0-13 | Minimal |
| 14-19 | Mild |
| 20-28 | Moderate |
| 29-63 | Severe |

**Psychometrics**:
- Cronbach's α = .92-.93
- Test-retest r = .93
- Convergent validity with HAM-D: r = .71

**Note**: Proprietary, requires purchase

### Center for Epidemiologic Studies Depression Scale (CES-D)

**Purpose**: Epidemiological depression screening

**Items**: 20 items

**Response Format**: 0-3 (frequency in past week)

**Scoring**: Sum (0-60), cut-off typically 16

**Psychometrics**:
- Cronbach's α = .85-.90
- Sensitivity: 80-90%
- Specificity: 70-80%

**Advantage**: Public domain

## Anxiety Measures

### Generalized Anxiety Disorder-7 (GAD-7)

**Purpose**: Screen for and measure anxiety severity

**Items**: 7 items

**Response Format**: 0-3 scale

**Scoring**:
| Score | Severity |
|-------|----------|
| 0-4 | Minimal |
| 5-9 | Mild |
| 10-14 | Moderate |
| 15-21 | Severe |

**Psychometrics**:
- Cronbach's α = .92
- Test-retest r = .83
- Sensitivity: 89%
- Specificity: 82%

### State-Trait Anxiety Inventory (STAI)

**Purpose**: Distinguish state (momentary) vs. trait (general) anxiety

**Items**: 40 items (20 state, 20 trait)

**Response Format**: 1-4 scale

**Scoring**: Separate scores for state and trait (20-80 each)

**Psychometrics**:
- Cronbach's α = .86-.95
- Test-retest (trait): r = .73-.86
- Test-retest (state): r = .16-.62

**Note**: Proprietary

### Beck Anxiety Inventory (BAI)

**Purpose**: Measure severity of anxiety symptoms

**Items**: 21 items

**Response Format**: 0-3 scale

**Scoring**:
| Score | Severity |
|-------|----------|
| 0-7 | Minimal |
| 8-15 | Mild |
| 16-25 | Moderate |
| 26-63 | Severe |

**Psychometrics**:
- Cronbach's α = .92
- Test-retest r = .75

## Combined Depression/Anxiety

### Depression Anxiety Stress Scales (DASS-21)

**Purpose**: Measure depression, anxiety, and stress

**Items**: 21 items (7 per subscale)

**Response Format**: 0-3 scale

**Scoring**: Multiply subscale sum by 2
| | Depression | Anxiety | Stress |
|---|---|---|---|
| Normal | 0-9 | 0-7 | 0-14 |
| Mild | 10-13 | 8-9 | 15-18 |
| Moderate | 14-20 | 10-14 | 19-25 |
| Severe | 21-27 | 15-19 | 26-33 |
| Extremely Severe | 28+ | 20+ | 34+ |

**Psychometrics**:
- Depression α = .94
- Anxiety α = .87
- Stress α = .91

**Advantage**: Free to use

### Hospital Anxiety and Depression Scale (HADS)

**Purpose**: Screen for anxiety and depression in medical settings

**Items**: 14 items (7 anxiety, 7 depression)

**Response Format**: 0-3 scale

**Scoring**: Subscale scores 0-21
| Score | Interpretation |
|-------|----------------|
| 0-7 | Normal |
| 8-10 | Borderline |
| 11-21 | Abnormal |

**Advantage**: Avoids somatic symptoms that may confound in medical populations

## Personality Measures

### Big Five Inventory-2 (BFI-2)

**Purpose**: Assess five factor model personality

**Domains**:
1. Extraversion
2. Agreeableness
3. Conscientiousness
4. Negative Emotionality (Neuroticism)
5. Open-Mindedness

**Items**: 60 items (12 per domain, 4 per facet)

**Facets per Domain**:
- Extraversion: Sociability, Assertiveness, Energy Level
- Agreeableness: Compassion, Respectfulness, Trust
- Conscientiousness: Organization, Productiveness, Responsibility
- Negative Emotionality: Anxiety, Depression, Emotional Volatility
- Open-Mindedness: Intellectual Curiosity, Aesthetic Sensitivity, Creative Imagination

**Psychometrics**:
- Domain α = .80-.90
- Facet α = .72-.80

### Ten-Item Personality Inventory (TIPI)

**Purpose**: Brief Big Five assessment

**Items**: 10 items (2 per domain)

**Use**: When time is extremely limited

**Psychometrics**:
- α = .68-.72 (lower due to few items)
- Convergent validity with BFI: r = .65-.87

### HEXACO Personality Inventory

**Purpose**: Assess six-factor model

**Domains**:
1. Honesty-Humility (unique to HEXACO)
2. Emotionality
3. Extraversion
4. Agreeableness
5. Conscientiousness
6. Openness to Experience

**Items**: 60, 100, or 200-item versions

**Psychometrics**:
- Domain α = .80-.90

## Well-Being Measures

### Satisfaction With Life Scale (SWLS)

**Purpose**: Assess global life satisfaction

**Items**: 5 items

**Response Format**: 1-7 scale

**Scoring**: Sum (5-35)
| Score | Interpretation |
|-------|----------------|
| 31-35 | Extremely satisfied |
| 26-30 | Satisfied |
| 21-25 | Slightly satisfied |
| 20 | Neutral |
| 15-19 | Slightly dissatisfied |
| 10-14 | Dissatisfied |
| 5-9 | Extremely dissatisfied |

**Psychometrics**:
- Cronbach's α = .87
- Test-retest r = .82

**Advantage**: Brief, public domain

### Positive and Negative Affect Schedule (PANAS)

**Purpose**: Measure positive and negative affect

**Items**: 20 items (10 positive, 10 negative)

**Response Format**: 1-5 scale

**Timeframes**: Moment, today, past week, past year, general

**Psychometrics**:
- Positive Affect α = .86-.90
- Negative Affect α = .84-.87
- PA-NA correlation: r = -.12 to -.23 (relatively independent)

### Warwick-Edinburgh Mental Well-being Scale (WEMWBS)

**Purpose**: Measure positive mental well-being

**Items**: 14 items (WEMWBS) or 7 items (SWEMWBS)

**Response Format**: 1-5 scale

**Scoring**: Sum of items

**Psychometrics**:
- Cronbach's α = .89-.91
- Test-retest r = .83

**Advantage**: Free to use with registration

### Psychological Well-Being Scales (PWB)

**Purpose**: Assess eudaimonic well-being dimensions

**Dimensions** (Ryff):
1. Autonomy
2. Environmental Mastery
3. Personal Growth
4. Positive Relations
5. Purpose in Life
6. Self-Acceptance

**Items**: 42 or 84-item versions

**Psychometrics**:
- Subscale α = .86-.93

## Cognitive Measures

### Montreal Cognitive Assessment (MoCA)

**Purpose**: Screen for mild cognitive impairment

**Domains**: Memory, attention, language, visuospatial, executive function

**Administration**: 10 minutes

**Scoring**: 0-30, cut-off 26

**Psychometrics**:
- Sensitivity for MCI: 90%
- Specificity: 87%

**Note**: Training required

### Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE)

**Purpose**: Cognitive screening

**Domains**: Orientation, registration, attention, recall, language

**Scoring**: 0-30
| Score | Interpretation |
|-------|----------------|
| 27-30 | Normal |
| 21-26 | Mild impairment |
| 10-20 | Moderate impairment |
| 0-9 | Severe impairment |

**Limitation**: Ceiling effects, less sensitive to MCI

### Trail Making Test (TMT)

**Purpose**: Assess processing speed (A) and cognitive flexibility (B)

**Part A**: Connect numbers in order (1-2-3...)
**Part B**: Alternate numbers and letters (1-A-2-B-3-C...)

**Scoring**: Time to completion (seconds)

**Interpretation**: Age-adjusted norms

**Psychometrics**:
- Test-retest r = .79 (A), .89 (B)

### Stroop Color-Word Test

**Purpose**: Assess inhibitory control

**Conditions**:
1. Word reading (color words in black)
2. Color naming (colored rectangles)
3. Interference (color words in incongruent colors)

**Scoring**: Time or correct responses

**Stroop Effect**: Interference - Color naming

## Stress and Coping

### Perceived Stress Scale (PSS)

**Purpose**: Measure perceived stress

**Items**: 10 or 14 items

**Response Format**: 0-4 scale (frequency)

**Scoring**: Higher = more perceived stress

**Psychometrics**:
- Cronbach's α = .84-.86
- Test-retest r = .85

**Advantage**: Public domain

### Brief COPE

**Purpose**: Assess coping strategies

**Items**: 28 items (14 subscales, 2 items each)

**Subscales**:
- Active coping
- Planning
- Positive reframing
- Acceptance
- Humor
- Religion
- Emotional support
- Instrumental support
- Self-distraction
- Denial
- Venting
- Substance use
- Behavioral disengagement
- Self-blame

**Scoring**: Subscale means or sums

**Groupings**:
- Problem-focused: Active, planning, instrumental support
- Emotion-focused: Emotional support, positive reframing, acceptance, religion, humor
- Dysfunctional: Denial, substance use, behavioral disengagement, self-blame, venting

### Connor-Davidson Resilience Scale (CD-RISC)

**Purpose**: Measure resilience

**Items**: 25 (full), 10, or 2-item versions

**Response Format**: 0-4 scale

**Psychometrics**:
- Cronbach's α = .89
- Test-retest r = .87

## Social and Interpersonal

### UCLA Loneliness Scale

**Purpose**: Assess loneliness

**Items**: 20 items (3-item short version available)

**Response Format**: 1-4 scale (frequency)

**Psychometrics**:
- Cronbach's α = .89-.94
- Test-retest r = .73

### Rosenberg Self-Esteem Scale (RSES)

**Purpose**: Measure global self-esteem

**Items**: 10 items (5 positive, 5 reverse-scored)

**Response Format**: 1-4 scale

**Scoring**: Sum (10-40), higher = more self-esteem

**Psychometrics**:
- Cronbach's α = .77-.88
- Test-retest r = .82-.88

**Advantage**: Public domain

### Experiences in Close Relationships (ECR)

**Purpose**: Assess adult attachment

**Dimensions**:
- Anxiety (fear of rejection/abandonment)
- Avoidance (discomfort with closeness)

**Items**: 36 items (18 per dimension)

**Response Format**: 1-7 scale

**Interpretation**:
- Secure: Low anxiety, low avoidance
- Preoccupied: High anxiety, low avoidance
- Dismissing: Low anxiety, high avoidance
- Fearful: High anxiety, high avoidance

**Psychometrics**:
- Subscale α = .90-.94

### Interpersonal Reactivity Index (IRI)

**Purpose**: Measure empathy

**Subscales**:
1. Perspective Taking (cognitive)
2. Fantasy (cognitive)
3. Empathic Concern (affective)
4. Personal Distress (affective)

**Items**: 28 items (7 per subscale)

**Psychometrics**:
- Subscale α = .71-.77

## Clinical Measures

### PTSD Checklist for DSM-5 (PCL-5)

**Purpose**: Screen for and measure PTSD symptoms

**Items**: 20 items (DSM-5 criteria)

**Response Format**: 0-4 scale

**Scoring**: Sum (0-80), cut-off 31-33

**Psychometrics**:
- Cronbach's α = .94
- Test-retest r = .82

**Advantage**: Free

### Alcohol Use Disorders Identification Test (AUDIT)

**Purpose**: Screen for hazardous alcohol use

**Items**: 10 items

**Scoring**:
| Score | Interpretation |
|-------|----------------|
| 0-7 | Low risk |
| 8-15 | Hazardous |
| 16-19 | Harmful |
| 20+ | Possible dependence |

**Psychometrics**:
- Sensitivity: 92%
- Specificity: 94%

### Insomnia Severity Index (ISI)

**Purpose**: Assess insomnia severity

**Items**: 7 items

**Response Format**: 0-4 scale

**Scoring**:
| Score | Interpretation |
|-------|----------------|
| 0-7 | No clinically significant insomnia |
| 8-14 | Subthreshold insomnia |
| 15-21 | Clinical insomnia (moderate) |
| 22-28 | Clinical insomnia (severe) |

**Psychometrics**:
- Cronbach's α = .74-.91
